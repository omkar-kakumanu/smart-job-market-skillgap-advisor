# Candidate & User Manual

## 1. Getting Started & Account Registration
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:5173/register`.
2. Enter your Full Name, Email, Password, and Target Role (e.g., *Full Stack Java Developer*).
3. Upon registration, you are automatically logged in and redirected to your Dashboard.

## 2. Managing Your Skill Inventory
1. Click **Profile** in the sidebar.
2. Under **Skill Inventory**, select a technical skill from the dropdown.
3. Select your proficiency level (*Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert*) and years of experience.
4. Click **Add to Profile**.

## 3. Running AI Skill Gap Analysis
1. Click **AI Advisor** in the top navigation or sidebar.
2. Choose an active industry job role from the dropdown, or type a custom role title.
3. Click **Analyze Skill Gap & Generate Roadmap**.
4. Review your **Skill Match %**, identified missing technical skills, and direct links to recommended courses.
